Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026. Applies to the Resume Rubric website and the Resume Rubric mobile app.

The short version: we do not collect anything about you. Your resume never leaves your device, and there are no accounts, no tracking and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details, and there is no sign up.

What happens to your resume

Your resume is read and scored on your own device, by code that is already inside the page or the app. It is never uploaded, never sent to us and never sent to anyone else. We could not read it even if we wanted to, because it never reaches us.

If you choose to save a version, it is stored on your own device only, in your browser's local storage or the app's own storage. You can delete all of it at any time using the "Delete everything" button in the Your data section. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser data, also removes it.

Files you open

PDF and Word files you import are read on your device to pull the text out. They are not uploaded. Files you export are written to your device and shared only if you choose to share them.

Website visit counts

The website counts how many times each page is opened, so we can tell how many people use it. That count is a number per day and nothing else. We do not record IP addresses, browser details, referrers, cookies or anything that could identify a visitor or link visits together. The counts are public at /stats. The mobile app does not do this at all.

The optional job search

The tool includes a job search you have to switch on yourself. When you press the button, your device asks public job board websites for their current listings. Those requests contain no information about you and no part of your resume. Job matching happens on your device after the listings arrive. If you never press the button, no request is ever made.

Those job boards are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies covering requests made to them.

Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies, no analytics service, no advertising pixels, no fingerprinting and no third party trackers of any kind.

Children

The tool is meant for people looking for work. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Sharing your information

We have no information about you to share, sell or disclose. There is no database.

Your rights

Laws such as GDPR and CCPA give you rights to see, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. The data created by using the tool is already yours, on your device, and you can delete it yourself in one click.

Security

Because nothing is transmitted or stored on our side, there is no server holding resumes that could be breached. Data on your device is protected by your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The tool is open source, so any change to how it handles data is visible publicly in its code.
